---
id: D-35
title: Partner web keeps its current domains, and EG manages DNS
useCase: uc2
resolves: [Q10]
---

Pages are served on the domains partner web uses today. DNS stays with EG
and points at EG's CDN, which forwards to Vercel as the origin of the new
front end.

*Why:* this is a change of CMS, not a move of the site. Keeping the domains
leaves no domain move for search engines to follow and nothing for visitors
to notice.
