AEM's header and footer keep the current brand. During coexistence a
visitor may see the new brand in a migrated business case and the current
brand elsewhere. This holds as long as the brand refresh has no fixed public
date before the last business case reaches 100% (Q27).

*Why:* reskinning AEM is new functionality on a platform being retired —
exactly the double development Q32 is about.
