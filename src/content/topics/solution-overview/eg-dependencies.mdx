<p class="sec-note">The plan holds if EG can provide what follows on time. Most of it is decisions and access rather than effort, and the effort falls on a few roles in a few phases.</p>

<h3>Constraints the plan works within</h3>
<ul class="tight">
  <li><strong>The AEM contract end does not move.</strong> Every partner web date is planned backwards from it (D-33).</li>
  <li><strong>The non-parity enhancements set the model.</strong> Content migrates once, into the enhanced model (D-27), so the design dates of the enhancements are on the critical path.</li>
  <li><strong>AEM stays in production until the last business case moves</strong>, with no new features from scope sign-off, and each business case's content frozen in both systems during its ramp (D-29).</li>
  <li><strong>The resolver runs on Akamai or as a Contentful app</strong> (D-09), on EG's accounts rather than EG's infrastructure, under EG's security and release processes.</li>
  <li><strong>EG's security requirements apply to the resolver and the front end</strong>, and EG reviews both before go-live.</li>
  <li><strong>NBA engineering was not at the workshop.</strong> What NBA has to do is logged for them in <a href="/sessions/nba-hand-off/">Session 2</a>, and proof points do not go live without it.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Access EG provides</h3>
<ul class="tight">
  <li><strong>Contentful</strong>: the organisation and the two spaces, with roles for Globant.</li>
  <li><strong>AWS environments and Akamai</strong>: EG's AWS account for the front end and EG's Akamai account, with delegated access for Globant (D-34), and DNS (D-35).</li>
  <li><strong>The resolver's platform</strong>: EdgeWorkers on EG's Akamai account, or an app in EG's Contentful organisation, and intake into EG's log system.</li>
  <li><strong>Test environments</strong> in NBA, SFMC, CRM and VMC.</li>
  <li><strong>AEM</strong>: read access to content and the site index, for discovery and the migration pipeline.</li>
  <li><strong>Figma</strong>: the design files, including the non-parity enhancements.</li>
</ul>

<p>Three roles gate the plan more than any other:</p>
<ul class="tight">
  <li><strong>Web Ops</strong>, in discovery, training and every ramp. They decide what happens to each piece of content, learn to manage it in Contentful, and check every business case as it moves.</li>
  <li><strong>NBA engineering and the channel engineers</strong>, during the build. M2 needs their work in NBA, SFMC, CRM and VMC.</li>
  <li><strong>A native reviewer per locale</strong>, as proof points scale. No translated variant goes out without one.</li>
</ul>
