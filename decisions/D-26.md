title: Content scope is set in a joint discovery phase, and EG decides
useCase: uc2
resolves: []
---

Every URL goes through the disposition framework — keep or retire, then fit
to the target model — and ends as migrate, rebuild or retire in the
disposition log. Globant prepares the data and proposes; EG content owners
decide, business case by business case, with rules first and URL
exceptions second.

*Why:* a conscious decision for every piece of content keeps redundant and
outdated pages off the new platform, and the redirect map has to be built
from the same list anyway.
