---
id: D-27
title: Content migrates once, into the post-rebrand model
useCase: uc2
resolves: []
---

The target model is the UC2 model plus the structural changes of the 2027
brand refresh. Bulk migration starts once that model is frozen, which has to
happen before discovery closes. Keep or retire does not depend on the model,
so that gate runs from the first day of discovery.

*Why:* migrating into the current model and transforming again after the
refresh would move the same content twice. The cost is a dependency: if the
refresh model slips, the migration slips with it (Q27, D-33).
