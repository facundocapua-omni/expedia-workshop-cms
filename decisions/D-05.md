---
id: D-05
title: Tone belongs to the partner; the channel only shapes the copy
useCase: uc1
resolves: []
---

Tone is the only voice in the model: a selection criterion chosen per partner
and constant across surfaces. A channel contributes no voice. Its profile fixes
the shape the copy must fit (length, allowed markup) and the `audience` it
addresses — partner for email and VMC, internal for CRM, which receives the
variant's briefing written in the same tone.

*Why:* the brief's step 5 requires tone to persist into the CRM view, and the
brief uses "tone" for two things that are not the same: the partner's framing,
and the way a surface speaks. The second is shape and audience, not voice.

*Reopens if:* EG confirms their operating model genuinely treats tone as
channel-determined (Q6b). Tone would then move to the channel profile, at the
cost of CMS-19, CMS-31 and CMS-06 — an explicit client decision.
