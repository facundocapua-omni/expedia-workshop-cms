---
id: D-29
title: A business case's content is frozen in both systems during its ramp
useCase: uc2
resolves: []
---

From the final delta migration until the business case reaches 100%, its
content is edited neither in AEM nor in Contentful. Urgent changes — legal,
pricing, errors — are made in both, by exception.

*Why:* rollback is only safe while AEM still serves current content. The
freeze keeps AEM a valid rollback target, and it is why ramps are kept short.
