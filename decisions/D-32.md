---
id: D-32
title: SEO protection — URLs kept, redirects at the edge, parity checked by crawl
useCase: uc2
resolves: []
---

A migrated page keeps its URL. Retired and consolidated URLs get a 301 to
their closest equivalent, or a 410 when there is none, and no chains. The
map comes from the disposition log, is served by the CDN and goes live when
the business case reaches 100%. On-page SEO is compared by crawling both
versions. A baseline is taken before discovery, and each business case is
monitored for four to six weeks after 100%.

*Why:* redirects at the edge reach every visitor during a ramp, whichever
origin serves them, and URL parity means most pages need no redirect at all.
