<p>Implementation comes last on purpose. The fields are decided from the design before anything is built, so the model is not shaped by one implementation, and the authoring manual is ready when the component reaches authors. A new component or a new variant goes through the same steps.</p>

<h3>The non-parity enhancements</h3>
<p>The non-parity enhancements, what partner web gains beyond parity with today's site, enter through the same flow. A visual change updates the design tokens, and pages follow without touching content. A change to what a component is made of goes back through the matrix and the content type, which is the case the <a href="/sessions/aem-migration/#content-scope">AEM migration</a> plans around (D-27).</p>
