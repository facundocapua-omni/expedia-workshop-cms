---
id: D-43
title: Accessibility is built into development
useCase: uc2
resolves: []
---

Accessibility is part of how components are built, as part of Globant's
agentic layer, rather than an audit once pages exist. Pages inherit it from
their components; what stays with the author, such as alternative text and
heading order, is covered in the authoring manual.

*Why:* an accessibility fix made once in a component reaches every page
that uses it, while an audit finds the same problem page by page.
