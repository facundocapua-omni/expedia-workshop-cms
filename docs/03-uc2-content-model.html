Use Case 2: partner web content on Contentful

The 35 approved components in the live inventory become 23 Contentful content types, of which 17 are blocks an author places on a page. This document carries the definitions and the decisions behind them; the accompanying workbook carries all 136 property specifications, filterable.

01Summary

35inventory components
26structural signatures
23content types
17authorable blocks
136properties specified
4kept distinct

The model takes shape in three moves. Five inventory entries share a content model with a parent and become properties on it — media position, background treatment, an optional CTA. Three collection containers share one model and become a single card collection with a layout property. And four entries belong to the structural layer rather than the page: a heading, a section wrapper, a separator and a button, which live on the section container or travel with their parent block.

Ten components become a content type of their own and are specified as they stand. The count was not driven down for its own sake: four further unifications were considered and the types kept distinct instead, and those four are set out in section 04 alongside the ones that were unified.

Every content type has a matching component in the Next.js and Tailwind front end built alongside it, so the 23 types resolve to 17 block components, one section wrapper and two route-level page components. The container decision pays off twice over there: presentation props become a single class map on one component instead of being restated seventeen times, and section.blocks resolves through one registry, so a new type later is a content-model change plus one registry entry.

Three types carry most of the delivery risk, and none of them is a modelling problem. The card collection, because query-driven collections are the least specified thing in any inventory. The lead capture form and the calculator, because their behaviour — progressive fields, submission, formula — is invisible from a rendered page and has to come from EG.

Three types carry L complexity — the card collection, the lead capture form and the calculator. Everything else is S or M, which is the shape you want: the risk is concentrated in three places rather than spread thinly across twenty-three.

04Modelling decisions

Ten decisions. Six bring components together under a single type; four keep types distinct where combining them would cost a validation or blur what an author reaches for. The four distinctions carry as much weight as the six unifications — together they show the count was reasoned rather than minimised.

Five types carry the three templates between them: the section container, the split banner, the card collection, the accordion and the video block. Six types appear on exactly one page — the comparison table, info boxes, form, calculator, testimonial and media card — which is expected at pilot scope and would be the first thing to re-test against the wider site.
