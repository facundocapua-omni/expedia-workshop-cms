---
id: D-08
title: Fallback content is authored, not hardcoded
useCase: uc1
resolves: []
---

Every proof point carries a mandatory default copy — its fallback —
translated and approved like any other content. It uses no variables and
makes no partner-specific claim. Email and web fail open with it; CRM fails closed.

*Why:* copy embedded in the resolver is the one piece of partner-facing
text nobody reviews, and a fallback per proof point says something
specific where a shared one could only say something generic.
