---
id: D-28
title: Coexistence is per business case, routed at the CDN
useCase: uc2
resolves: [Q11]
---

A business case is one or more sections, defined as a set of paths. The CDN
routes each business case to AEM or to the new front end, and a sticky
cookie keeps each visitor in one cohort. Every business case ramps through
wave 0 (internal), 20%, 50% and 100%, with an EG go/no-go between steps.
Rollback is a routing change at the edge that takes minutes; DNS does not
change.

*Why:* a business case that fails rolls back alone, and the cohort still on
AEM is the control group the conversion gate is measured against.
