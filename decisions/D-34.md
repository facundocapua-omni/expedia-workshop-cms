id: D-34
title: Globant builds and runs the partner web front end, on EG's Vercel account
useCase: uc2
resolves: [Q8]
---

The partner web front end is a Next.js and Tailwind application. Globant
builds it, hosts it on Vercel and runs it in production: deployments,
monitoring, dependency and security updates, and incidents. The Vercel
account and contract belong to EG, and Globant administers them with
delegated access.

*Why:* one party owns the front end end to end, and EG keeps the account
and the billing, so a change of supplier is a handover of access rather
than a hosting migration.
