AEM, its header and footer included, stays as it is. During coexistence a
visitor may see the non-parity enhancements in a migrated business case and
the current site elsewhere. This holds as long as the enhancements have no
fixed public date before the last business case reaches 100% (Q27).

*Why:* adding them to AEM is new functionality on a platform being retired —
exactly the double development Q32 is about.
