<p class="sec-note">Most of this topic is for EG to set: the requirements the new front end has to meet. Globant commits to two things up front, parity with what partner web does today and accessibility built into development.</p>

<div class="decide">
  <h4>Parity on SEO, tracking and consent</h4>
  <p>Search engines, analytics and visitors' consent choices should notice no change: whatever partner web does today on those three fronts, the new front end does too (D-42). How search is protected during cutover is covered in the <a href="/sessions/aem-migration/#cutover">AEM migration</a>, and tracking and SEO parity are gates of every ramp step.</p>
</div>

<div class="decide">
  <h4>Accessibility is built into development</h4>
  <p>Accessibility is part of how components are built, as part of Globant's agentic layer, rather than an audit once pages exist (D-43). Pages inherit it from their components; what stays with the author, such as alternative text and heading order, is covered in the authoring manual.</p>
</div>
