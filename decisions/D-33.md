id: D-33
title: The plan runs backwards from the AEM contract end; an extension is negotiated only if needed
useCase: uc2
resolves: []
---

The last business case reaches 100% no later than contract end minus buffer
minus rollback window. The first business case is the pilot's, whose model
and front end already exist. A checkpoint at contract end minus notice
period minus negotiation time decides, from the business cases still to
move, whether an AEM extension is negotiated.

*Why:* business case waves make a shortfall bounded and measurable, which is
what makes a short extension negotiable. Without a dated checkpoint, "only
if needed" is found out too late to act on.
