  <p class="sec-note">The design introduces one custom runtime component, which means it introduces something that has to be operated. Naming who does that now is cheaper than discovering it at go-live.</p>

  <p>Until the support owner is named, four things have no home: the service SLA, the on-call rota, the runbook, and the severity definitions that decide whether a rising fallback rate wakes someone at 3am or waits for Monday. None of them is hard to produce — each simply needs an owner first, and the usual failure is to leave the question until the service is already live.</p>
