Whatever partner web does today for search engines, for analytics and for
visitors' consent choices, the new front end does too. Requirements beyond
parity are EG's to set.

*Why:* a change of CMS should go unnoticed by search engines, by the
analytics and by visitors. Parity is also what the ramp gates measure at
every step of the migration (Q31).
