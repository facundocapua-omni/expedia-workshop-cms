<p class="sec-note">The plan has no dates yet, on purpose. Each milestone's date follows from an input EG holds, listed in the table below, and the partner web dates are planned backwards from the AEM contract end (D-33).</p>

<div class="decide">
  <h4><span class="stamp">proposal</span>One plan, from platform setup to the last business case</h4>
  <p>Platform setup comes first, and everything else builds on it. Discovery runs alongside the design of the non-parity enhancements, and closes only once the enhanced model is frozen (M3). Build starts when discovery closes (M4), and covers the models and authoring, the resolver and the channel integrations, and the front end, so the first proof points can go live (M2) while partner web is still being built.</p>
  <p>Content migration is a phase of its own: the pipeline, and the runs that move each business case before it ramps (D-31). Training comes before anyone goes live: authors and approvers, native reviewers and Web Ops learn the models, the approval gates and the live preview, with the authoring manual, proof point authors before M2 and Web Ops before the first business case ramps.</p>
  <p>Hypercare starts with the first go-live (M2) and ends a month after the last business case goes live (M7). After it, the platform moves into continuous enhancement, with 24/7 support if EG chooses it.</p>
</div>

<h3>What sets the pace</h3>
<ul class="tight">
  <li><strong>Partner web has the fixed end.</strong> Its critical path runs through the enhanced model (M3), then discovery (M4) and build. Once the pipeline is proven, how many ramps overlap is limited by EG's QA capacity and by how many content freezes can run at once.</li>
  <li><strong>Proof points are paced by decisions, not by build.</strong> The resolver is a small service, built once discovery closes (M4). What moves M2 is EG's choice between options A and B, the support owner, and the time NBA and the channel teams can give.</li>
  <li><strong>One link between proof points and partner web.</strong> Partner web shows proof points through the resolver, so that needs M2 and a business case live on the new front end. Neither waits for it.</li>
</ul>
