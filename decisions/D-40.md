---
id: D-40
title: Figma is the source of every component
useCase: uc2
resolves: []
---

A component is designed in Figma first. The component matrix, the content
type and the authoring manual are derived from that design, and the
component is implemented in Next.js after them, bound to its content
type.

*Why:* the design, the content model, the guidance and the code describe
the same component, and a change starts in one place instead of drifting
between four.
